# Does the story-period patch carry a winning-plan-slot code?

**Frozen before any double-flip score.** The [first-hop patch result](firsthop_swap_patch_result.md) found that two substitutions move the later name margin toward the other-world winner: swapping the route→link *world* (+0.600 nat) and reversing the order of the two named plans while keeping the correct person fixed (+0.866). Reversing only the first-hop fact-clause order was small (+0.079). The idea that both large arms flip a shared **winning listed-plan slot** was proposed after those scores. This test is conditional on that discovery and is a prediction of that idea, not independent discovery of a trope representation.

## Fixed grid, model, and states

Use the exact 128 target prompts in `firsthop_swap_v1.json`, SHA-256 `52a26471f27322ad18bc6f72d57d022ffa7f5372609b38bea855dc45570490be`, and the already-captured post-block-24 residual at the period ending the second-hop facts. The model remains NDIF's pinned `google/gemma-2-9b-it`, local tokenizer snapshot `11c9b309abf73637e4b6f9a3fa1e92e615547819`; NDIF exposes no weight revision hash. The old patch report is fixed by SHA-256 `914152d540c625a2caf2733bca8b368720b13f157e27008bcd4015d9b99e21cd`. Its observed arms calibrate the frozen decision and provide a replication reference, but the world and plan comparison arms are **rescored in this run**. Its 256 state arrays are ignored local files and must be copied byte-for-byte into this worktree, checked against their content hashes and the old report's per-cell state hashes. Refuse missing or mismatched states or report; do not silently re-extract or substitute another deployment.

For a target with `(domain, name_order=n, plan_order=p, world=w, goal=g)`, let `h_t` be its two-row post-block-24 period state. The **double-flip source** is `(domain,n,1−p,1−w,g)`. Its text changes both the route→link assignments and which named plan sentence comes first. The correct *person* changes under the world flip, while the correct *listed-plan position* stays the same because reversing plan order flips the position a second time. Assert this truth table by following the grid's plan→route→link→destination graph and by checking the source/target order fields, not only by an algebraic formula. Assert equal candidate names, prompt token length, period index and period token ID for every source–target pair, plus one BOS and zero candidate-row padding. The source and target hidden rows must each be bit-identical across the two candidates at the period.

Use two prespecified double-flip vectors at that one period token:

- **Natural:** `v_D = h_double − h_t`, the actual source-state substitution.
- **World-norm matched:** `v_M = v_D × ‖h_world − h_t‖ / ‖v_D‖`, where `h_world` is the other-world, same-plan state. This tests whether a small natural effect is merely a smaller perturbation. State norms were inspected before any double score: the median double/world norm ratio is 0.883, range 0.662–1.323, so the natural dose alone cannot adjudicate cancellation.

Both vectors are formed in float32 from the saved post-block-24 states, then written by type to **both candidate rows** at the target period. The score is the same verified, fixed two-candidate teacher-forced Gemma scorer and fp32 logit softcap as the prior run. Every new job is fingerprinted by exact prompt, candidates, period index and token ID, arm, patch layer, vector SHA, old report SHA, exact state-content SHAs, tokenizer revision and code digest. Cache rows per job and refuse stale or duplicate IDs.

## Measurement and controls

Score the full 128-prompt grid in one fixed batch shape per job with eight arms: `none`, `zero`, `world_natural` (the old other-world state delta), `plan_matched` (the old same-world reversed-plan state delta norm-matched to the world delta), `double_natural`, `double_matched`, a seeded isotropic random direction norm-matched to `v_M`, and the natural double vector applied at block 41 to the same period token. The fixed random seed is `20260924 + domain_index*16 + name_order*8 + plan_order*4 + goal*2 + world`, before unit normalization and float32 casting. Run `none` first. Its two candidate scores must agree with the committed old no-patch scores within 1e-3 **on all 128 prompts** before interpreting any new patch arm. Check the new scorer against the established core on the shortest and longest prompts before the grid. Re-capture the eight `(n,p,w,g)=(0,0,0,0)` states, as in the prior run, and require maximum relative drift ≤0.02. Every double source–target norm must exceed ten times that maximum times the target norm, as in the prior instrument gate.

Re-run the prior row-0-only negative preflight on one fixed shortest prompt: the armed moved-candidates assertion must refuse a patch that moves just row 0. Verify two-row reach for the natural double vector on that prompt and bf16 addition at the patched period. The core moved-candidates assertion stays armed for all five nonzero block-24 arms; use the prior deterministic two-rerun policy when bf16 scores are identical, record each flag, and fail the instrument if more than 5% of these 640 active jobs are flagged. Verify the natural source-patch state is within relative L2 0.02 of the source state on every prompt and that the matched patch equals the predicted bf16 addition. The `zero` and final-block same-period pass-through scores must be within 1e-3 of new `none` scores on every prompt. If an instrument gate fails, report it and leave the slot question unreadable. Do not change job size, layer, site or dose after scores.

The **newly scored** world and plan arms enter the primary paired contrasts with the new double arms. ## Frozen statistics and decisions

For each target, `m = logp(plan-A owner) − logp(plan-B owner)` and `s=+1` if its **other-world** winner is the plan-A owner, else `−1`, derived from the source graph. The double source has that same winner. For a new arm, `E_arm = s·(m_arm−m_none_new)`; old effects use the committed old no-patch. Average the 16 directed cells within each of eight domains. The eight domains are the inference units; report 64 unordered world-pair means separately. Use one-sided exact 2^8 domain sign-flip tests with identity included and a fixed-seed 10,000-draw domain bootstrap for directed contrasts. For near-zero decisions use a two-sided 95% domain-bootstrap interval. Report all 128 cell effects, domain values, name/plan/world/goal halves, **leave-one-domain-out means for both double arms**, absolute-margin shrinkage, source-winner top-name flips, and the 19 previously source-favoured baseline cells.

The **slot-cancellation screen** passes only if all instrument gates pass and all of these hold:

1. Both natural and world-norm-matched double means lie in [−0.15,+0.15] nat, and both bootstrap 95% intervals lie wholly inside [−0.40,+0.40]. This equivalence margin was calibrated **before any double score** on the committed, slot-preserving first-hop clause-order arm: its mean is +0.079 and its domain-bootstrap interval is approximately [−0.127,+0.337], so that reference would pass. The new random arm is separately reported. This is an equivalence gate, not a failure to reject a nonzero effect.
2. The **within-run** paired `E_world_natural − E_double_matched` contrast has mean ≥+0.25 nat, exact one-sided p≤0.05, bootstrap lower bound >0 and at least six positive domains.
3. The **within-run** paired `E_plan_matched − E_double_matched` contrast passes the same four conditions. Both plan and double arms are norm-matched to the natural world delta.

If it passes, read the result as support for cancellation of a **winning listed-plan-slot component** at this token, under these constructed prompts. It does not establish a unique slot vector, a complete route graph code, or a story edit. A double effect that remains positive at both doses, with mean ≥+0.25 nat, exact p≤0.05, bootstrap lower >0 and at least six positive domains, **fails the cancellation prediction** and supports an answer-relevant person/world component or a more general perturbation account. If neither screen passes, report a mixed or unresolved outcome. A materially negative double effect outside the equivalence band also fails cancellation, with its sign and controls reported rather than converted to a positive by absolute value.

The two old single-flip effects may be nonadditive because downstream attention, RMSNorm and softcap are nonlinear. Do not infer a circuit identity from `E_world + E_plan − E_double`. The period patch also leaves the target's earlier visible text unchanged, so any positive effect may operate through an inconsistent state. All claim wording remains scoped to this preselected period, block and eight-domain grid.

# Deprecated: `claude/` and `human/` conscription grids

**As of hour 62 these grids are not used for new work.** Every shame-axis experiment from hour 62
on runs on a versioned snapshot of the paper's own stimuli in `stimuli/`, modified in targeted,
logged edits.

Why: every conscription result (hours 54–61) rested on items written by the same model that was
designing the experiments, on both grids, in one voice. The paper's own items remove that
authorship confound and make every number directly comparable to theirs.

The files stay. Hours 54–61, their results and their claims were measured on them, and a record
you can't re-run isn't a record. They are not to be extended, and no new result may be drawn
from them. The one question the paper's set cannot answer — record vs. conversation, which needs
claims with checkable content (their `gaslighting` items mostly have none) — is recorded as
answered on grid 2 only (hour 59), with that limit stated.
